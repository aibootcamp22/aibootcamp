The Journey Begins

Day 1: October 19th - The sacred pilgrimage commences at Yeshvantpura Railway Station. Gather at 11:00 AM for a timely departure. The Karnataka Sampark Kranti Express awaits at 1:00 PM.

Through the Heart of India

Day 2: October 20th - A full day on the rails, traversing from South to North India. Watch landscapes transform outside your window and share stories with fellow pilgrims.

Delhi's Imperial Grandeur

Day 3: October 21st - Arrive in Delhi. Explore monuments that whisper ancient stories: India Gate, Qutub Minar, and Humayun's Tomb.

Golden Serenity Awaits

Day 4: October 22nd - Amritsar. Visit the Golden Temple, Jallianwala Bagh, and witness the Wagah Border ceremony.

Ascending to Mountain Bliss

Day 5: October 23rd - Journey from Amritsar to the pine-scented air of Dalhousie. The Dhauladhar mountains embrace you.

Switzerland of India

Day 6: October 24th - Explore Khajjiar, the "Mini Switzerland," before journeying to Katra, the base camp for your pilgrimage.

The Sacred Ascent

Day 7, October 25th - Jai Mata Di! The day of ultimate devotion arrives. Your 13km trek to Mata Vaishno Devi Bhawan unfolds.

Rest & Reflection

Day 8: October 26th - A day to rest your body and integrate the soul's experience. Explore Katra's vibrant market.

Homeward with Grace

Day 9, October 27th - Depart from Jammu, carrying the Mother's grace, and begin your journey home to Bengaluru.
